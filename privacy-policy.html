Boom Squad – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Boom Squad ("the game", "we") collects, why, and what choices you have. The game is published by Dean Bozinoski. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at d.bozinoski@gmail.com.

1. Playing without an account

You can play Boom Squad without signing in. In that case your progress (level, coins, weapons, campaign progress and similar game data) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

2. Information we collect

InformationWhenWhy
Google account identifier, and the name and email address of your Google account Only if you choose "Sign in with Google" To create your account and recognise you on other devices. We never see your Google password.
Player name and avatar you choose When you create or edit your profile To show your profile in the game. Player names are unique, so a name you choose cannot be used by another player.
Game progress (level, XP, coins, scrap, weapons, upgrades, campaign progress, battle statistics, energy and reward timers) While you are signed in To save your progress to the cloud so you can continue on another device.
Google Play Games gamer tag and player ID If you use Google Play Games To connect the game with your Play Games profile.
Advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data When ads are loaded or shown Collected by Google AdMob to show rewarded ads, measure them and prevent fraud. See section 4.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or any payment information. The game has no in-app purchases.

3. Services we use

The game relies on the following services provided by Google, which process data on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own policies:

  • Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google LLC) – account sign-in and cloud save. Firebase privacy information
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) – rewarded video ads. How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Play Games Services (Google LLC) – optional Play Games profile.
  • Google Sign-In / Credential Manager (Google LLC) – signing in with your Google account.

Google's general privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Ads and your consent

Boom Squad shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one (for example to double a reward or refill energy). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can refuse, and you will then see non-personalised ads. You can also reset or limit your Advertising ID at any time in your device's settings (Settings › Google › Ads or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

5. How long we keep data

Account and cloud-save data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, player name and cloud save are deleted. Data processed by AdMob is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time inside the game: open the player badge on the main menu, then Account › Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, player name and cloud save. Progress stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the game. You can also ask us to delete your data by writing to d.bozinoski@gmail.com.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or California privacy law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at d.bozinoski@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Cloud data is stored in Google Cloud and protected by access rules so that each player can read and change only their own data.

9. Children

Boom Squad is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the game.
